# Compression of Qualitative Variation: Replication Package

Research data and materials for the study *Compression of Qualitative Variation: The Limits of Prompt Complexity in LLM-Generated Landing Pages*, submitted to the **Journal on Interactive Systems (JIS)**.

This repository contains everything required to understand, audit, replicate or extend the study: the prompts, the skill files, the source code of the nine generated artifacts exactly as delivered by the models, the evaluation instrument, the four independent evaluation records, and the consolidation and agreement analysis.

---

## Study overview

Nine landing pages for a fictional real-estate application were generated by three large language models under three levels of prompt complexity. Four researchers independently evaluated the resulting artifacts using an instrument of 24 criteria organized into 6 dimensions.

| | Condition 1 (simple) | Condition 2 (skills) | Condition 3 (detailed + skills) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini 3 Flash** | `artifacts/gemini-3-flash/condition-1-simple` | `condition-2-skills` | `condition-3-detailed` |
| **GPT-OSS 120B** | `artifacts/gpt-oss-120b/condition-1-simple` | `condition-2-skills` | `condition-3-detailed` |
| **Claude Opus 4.6** | `artifacts/claude-opus-4-6/condition-1-simple` | `condition-2-skills` | `condition-3-detailed` |

Design: Design Science Research (DSR), six activities.
Evaluation: four human evaluators, 216 assignments each, 864 in total.
Agreement: full agreement in 51.6% of 192 valid records, rising to 77.6% when agreement among at least three evaluators is considered.

### Naming convention

All files follow `scope_description_version.ext`, in lowercase, with hyphens inside each segment and underscores between segments. Model identifiers are `gemini-3-flash`, `gpt-oss-120b` and `claude-opus-4-6`. Conditions are `condition-1-simple`, `condition-2-skills` and `condition-3-detailed`. Evaluators are identified as `evaluator-01` through `evaluator-04`; the mapping to their initials, the normalization of the response scales, and the full register of omitted and out-of-scale responses are given in `docs/data-dictionary.md`.

## Integrity of the artifacts

The source code in `artifacts/` is preserved exactly as delivered by each model. Configuration failures, missing dependencies and incomplete files were deliberately **not** corrected, since they are themselves objects of analysis (criteria 14 and 15). Do not open pull requests fixing them.

Six artifacts were structured in Vite, two in plain HTML/JS and one in Next.js. To equalize inspection conditions, all nine were deployed to a hosting platform before evaluation; the rendering conditions are described in the paper.

---

## How to reproduce the evaluation

1. Serve or deploy each artifact in `artifacts/` without modifying its code.
2. Open `instrument/` and apply the 24 criteria to each of the nine interfaces.
3. Record one file per evaluator, following the schema in `docs/data-dictionary.md`.
4. Consolidate: dichotomous criteria by simple majority; ordinal, count and five-point criteria by the median of the four assignments, adopting the lower point when the median falls between two scale points. Cells with an even split are marked `DIV`.

Criterion 07 is descriptive and receives specific treatment: equivalent terms are normalized (for example, "features" and "resources", or "testimonials" and "reviews"), and only sections named by at least two evaluators are consolidated.

### This repository

This replication package was assembled with the assistance of **Claude Opus 5** (Anthropic), under the supervision of the authors. Specifically, the model:

- converted the four original evaluator spreadsheets into the long-format CSV files in `evaluations/raw/`, translating the response scales from Portuguese into the English scales of the instrument;
- applied the consolidation rules to produce `consolidated-results.csv` and recomputed the inter-evaluator agreement indices, both validated cell by cell against the values published in the article;
- recovered the skill files from the artifact repositories and verified that the six copies were byte-identical;
- captured the screenshots of the nine deployed artifacts;
- organized the folder structure and the file naming convention;
- drafted the explanatory texts in this README, in `docs/data-dictionary.md`, and in the two `PROVENANCE.md` files.

No evaluation value was assigned, altered or imputed by the model. The four independent evaluations, the instrument and the consolidation rules are the work of the authors, and every derived figure in this repository was checked against the article before publication. Responsibility for the content rests entirely with the authors.
